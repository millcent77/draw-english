# Complete sung teaching recordings

All three MP3s are complete, unmodified educational recordings published for download by American English, Office of English Language Programs, U.S. Department of State, in **Sing Out Loud Children's Songs**. The resource page explicitly provides MP3 download links and classroom teaching materials. Source: https://americanenglish.state.gov/resources/sing-out-loud-childrens-songs

Files were downloaded from these official educational download links without audio conversion, voice generation, pitch processing or editing. Traditional English lyrics are displayed with Chinese translations written for this project. The downloaded recordings retain their source attribution and are not relabeled as Creative Commons or licensed as application code. No endorsement by the publisher is implied. Metadata and SHA-256 digests are recorded in recordings.json.

The former short Wikimedia Ogg demonstration recordings were removed. A YouTube-player approach was tested but could not load on the target computer, so the shipped implementation uses these local teaching-song files. It never substitutes spoken speech for a failed song. Approximate line cues are learning hints, not verified karaoke alignment. The third selection is now The Alphabet Song rather than the old Row tune sample.
